## BSCpE program outcomes (CMO 87 Annex II, "PROGRAM OUTCOMES")

CMO 87 lists 12 outcomes, labeled (a) through (l). These follow the ABET EC-2000 (a)–(k) graduate-outcome pattern with a Philippine-specific (l) on engineering/management principles. Clause IDs use the CMO's own letter labels.

- **bscpe-po-a** — Ability to apply knowledge of mathematics and science to solve complex engineering problems.
- **bscpe-po-b** — Ability to design and conduct experiments, as well as to analyze and interpret data.
- **bscpe-po-c** — Ability to design a system, component, or process to meet desired needs within realistic constraints such as economic, environmental, social, political, ethical, health and safety, manufacturability, and sustainability, in accordance with standards.
- **bscpe-po-d** — Ability to function on multidisciplinary teams.
- **bscpe-po-e** — Ability to identify, formulate, and solve complex engineering problems.
- **bscpe-po-f** — Understanding of professional and ethical responsibility.
- **bscpe-po-g** — Ability to communicate effectively.
- **bscpe-po-h** — Broad education necessary to understand the impact of engineering solutions in a global, economic, environmental, and societal context.
- **bscpe-po-i** — Recognition of the need for, and an ability to engage in, life-long learning.
- **bscpe-po-j** — Knowledge of contemporary issues.
- **bscpe-po-k** — Ability to use techniques, skills, and modern engineering tools necessary for engineering practice.
- **bscpe-po-l** — Knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments. *(CHED-specific addition beyond the ABET EC-2000 (a)–(k) core.)*

## FEU Tech institutional specialization tracks (NOT enumerated in CMO 87)

CMO 87's Annex II sample-curriculum map lists only "Cognate / Track Course 1/2/3" as placeholders — it does **not** enumerate named specialization tracks. FEU Tech publishes two BSCpE tracks on its academics page (https://www.feutech.edu.ph/academics/bscpe): **Internet of Things and Data Analytics** and **Network Administration and Cybersecurity**. The two clauses below are **FEU-Tech-institutional, not CMO-authoritative**; they cross-walk to the CMO outcomes they primarily exercise.

- **bscpe-sp-iot** — IoT / embedded-systems specialization. Exercises `bscpe-po-c`, `bscpe-po-e`, `bscpe-po-k`, `bscpe-po-j`.
- **bscpe-sp-net** — Network administration / cybersecurity specialization. Exercises `bscpe-po-c`, `bscpe-po-f`, `bscpe-po-h`, `bscpe-po-k`.
