## Responsibility level scale (shared across all SFIA skills)

Canonical: https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9/responsibilities

- **Level 1 — Follow.** Works under close supervision on routine tasks.
- **Level 2 — Assist.** Performs varied activities with occasional direction.
- **Level 3 — Apply.** Performs a range of work in different contexts under general direction.
- **Level 4 — Enable.** Performs a broad range of complex technical/professional activities; substantial personal responsibility and autonomy.
- **Level 5 — Ensure/Advise.** Provides authoritative guidance; leads on technical/professional decisions.
- **Level 6 — Initiate/Influence.** Has defined authority and accountability; influences significant organizational direction.
- **Level 7 — Set strategy/Inspire.** Sets direction at the highest organizational levels.

For undergraduate evidence, realistic levels are **1–3**. Reserve **level 4+** for clearly documented evidence of professional-authority work.

## How to cite

Use `[sfia-9:<CODE>-<LEVEL>]`, e.g. `[sfia-9:PROG-3]` for Apply-level programming, `[sfia-9:DTAN-2]` for Assist-level data modelling and design. The level MUST be an integer 1–7.

Default to **PROG-1 / PROG-2** for students whose evidence is coursework-only. Reserve **PROG-3** for external-facing, independent work (internship, maintained open-source contribution, extended personal project with users). Never exceed **PROG-4** for an undergraduate without clearly documented team-lead or professional-authority evidence.
